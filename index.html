I build deep learning models for medical images, and I care about making their decisions understandable to the clinicians who would rely on them.

Research interests: explainable AI and medical image analysis, in particular prototype-based reasoning, attribution methods (LIME) and multi-task models for segmentation and severity grading. I also work on NLP: my final-year project was named entity recognition for heliophysics literature (ModernBERT, FLAN-T5).

I am a software engineer (École polytechnique privée de Monastir, ranked first in my class) with a research master's in Software Engineering specialised in deep learning (ISIMM, ranked second in my class). In 2025 I worked at BASIRA Lab in London on explainable models for Parkinson's disease diagnosis from spiral drawings. I am a member of MICCAI and currently a part-time lecturer at ISIMM.

Looking for a PhD position in explainable AI and medical image analysis (interpretable diagnosis, segmentation and severity grading, trustworthy medical ML), in France or elsewhere in Europe. I am also open to adjacent topics such as NLP and trustworthy ML.
